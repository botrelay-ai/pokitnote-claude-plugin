description: Sign in to Pokit Note so Claude can use your notes, lists, and recipes.
---

Help the person sign in to Pokit Note.

If the Pokit Note tools already work, say they are signed in and stop.

Otherwise tell them, in these words or close to them:

1. Type `/mcp` and press Return.
2. Choose **pokitnote**, then choose **Authenticate**.
3. A Pokit Note page opens in your browser. Sign in, or create an account, then allow access to notes, lists, and recipes.
4. Come back here when the page says you're done.

Do not ask for, copy, or use the key saved by the Pokit Note app on the iPhone.
